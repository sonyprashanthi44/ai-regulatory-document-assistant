ACME CORP — INFORMATION SECURITY & DATA RETENTION POLICY
Version 3.2 | Effective Date: 2025-01-15 | Owner: Chief Compliance Officer

1. Purpose
This policy defines how ACME Corp protects company and customer data and how long records are retained.

2. Data classification
- Public: marketing materials, public website content
- Internal: employee directories, internal wikis
- Confidential: customer PII, contracts, financials
- Restricted: authentication secrets, encryption keys, M&A documents

3. Data retention
- Customer account records: retained for 7 years after account closure
- Security incident logs: retained for 3 years
- Employee HR files: retained for 5 years after separation
- Marketing analytics (non-PII): retained for 24 months
- Backup tapes: rotated on a 90-day cycle

4. Access control
Access to Confidential and Restricted data requires manager approval and quarterly access reviews.
Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for all remote access to production systems.
Shared admin accounts are prohibited.

5. Incident response
Suspected data breaches must be reported to the Security Operations Center within 1 hour of discovery.
The CISO and Legal must be notified within 4 hours for incidents involving customer PII.
Affected customers are notified within 72 hours when legally required.

6. Vendor management
Vendors processing Confidential data must sign a Data Processing Agreement (DPA).
Vendors are reassessed annually. Critical vendors require SOC 2 Type II or equivalent.

7. Acceptable use
Company devices must use full-disk encryption.
Employees must not upload Confidential or Restricted documents to unsanctioned generative AI tools.
Personal cloud storage is not approved for company Confidential data.

8. Exceptions
Exceptions require written approval from the Chief Compliance Officer and expire after 90 days unless renewed.
